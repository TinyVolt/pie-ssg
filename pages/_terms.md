## Gradient
The vector of partial derivatives of a function, $\nabla f = (\partial f / \partial x_1, \dots, \partial f / \partial x_n)$. It points in the direction in which the function grows fastest, and its length is how fast it grows in that direction.

At a minimum or maximum, the gradient is zero. Gradient descent uses this: it steps against the gradient, again and again, until the steps get tiny.
